# Vooya Lab identity exploration brief

Create a concept sheet for a distinctive open-source developer laboratory named
“Vooya Lab”. The product turns bounded Rust/WASM capabilities into interactive,
inspectable Web components. It is not a crypto product, gaming brand, generic
AI startup, or cyberpunk dashboard.

Explore a modular identity family around the seed `V/`: the V suggests Vooya;
the slash suggests a source path, an integration boundary, and forward motion.
Show several genuinely different construction systems rather than color swaps.

Initial hypotheses to separate on the contact sheet:

- **Boundary Cut** — one precise slash cuts or joins two planes, expressing the
  typed host/Rust boundary with the fewest possible shapes;
- **Spectral Trace** — a restrained trace crosses the V and reveals a cyan or
  violet spectral layer only at the boundary;
- **Indexed Monogram** — V and slash emerge from grid coordinates, crop marks,
  or compiler-style indexing rather than conventional logo lettering; and
- **Living Slash** — a static micro mark whose motion study turns the slash into
  a compile/progress/success transition without changing the core silhouette.

Visual character:

- experimental editorial design meets scientific instrument;
- near-black green substrate;
- acid chartreuse, spectral cyan, controlled violet, and rare coral error signal;
- hard grids, orbital crops, trace lines, indexing marks, and precise negative
  space;
- high craft, restrained, memorable at 16 px.

Required studies:

1. micro mark at favicon/navigation size;
2. horizontal wordmark relationship;
3. square repository/social avatar;
4. monochrome construction;
5. one motion-frame idea for compile/build completion;
6. a small material/pattern library that can support case covers.

Avoid gradients that resemble generic Web3 branding, circuit-brain imagery,
Rust gear mascots, literal browser windows, fake code, illegible AI lettering,
soft glassmorphism, and copying any reference logo. Typography may be indicated
compositionally, but the final wordmark will be redrawn as vector type.

Output should be a clean design-board/contact-sheet presentation with generous
negative space and labels for the concept families.
